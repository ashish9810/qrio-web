# Qrio blog plan

This file drives the daily blog drafts. Each morning one post is drafted from the top of the queue, saved as a draft in `content/blog/`, and reviewed with Ashish in chat. Nothing is published until Ashish says so.

## Goal

Rank on Google and get cited by AI assistants for people who want an alternative to doomscrolling, and for people who want to stay informed on business, startups and the world quickly. Every post ends with the Qrio call to action (added automatically). Qrio is a free Android app of short videos on business, startups and the world. We do not host video on the website, so posts are text only.

## Reader

Young working professionals and students in Indian cities (23 to 32): tech, startups, finance, consulting, top colleges. Curious, busy, a little tired of their feeds.

## Editorial rules (the checker enforces most of these)

- Calm, smart, warm, plain English. Short sentences. Never preachy.
- No em dashes anywhere. No full stop at the end of any heading or title.
- Never use the words: learn, educational, explainer, knowledge, upskill, intellectual, Reels. (Words inside URLs are fine.)
- Open with a direct answer in the first two sentences. Then headings that match real questions people type.
- Every number, study or claim must come from a source that was actually opened and read. Never write a statistic from memory. If a figure cannot be verified, leave it out. Say plainly when research is only correlational.
- Link sources inline and list them under `## Sources`.
- Prefer primary sources (the paper, the report, the official site) over blog summaries.
- Be fair to competitors. Balanced lists are cited more and are more honest. If Qrio is mentioned, add a one-line disclosure that we make it.
- 1,100 to 1,700 words. Include one table or list where it helps. Add 3 or 4 FAQ items in the frontmatter, each answer under 60 words.
- Link to other published Qrio posts where relevant (use relative links like `/blog/slug`).
- India context where it is real and sourced. Do not force it.
- Frontmatter: title, description (120 to 160 characters), date, category, status: draft, faq.
- Categories: Doomscrolling, Staying informed, Explained, Behind Qrio.
