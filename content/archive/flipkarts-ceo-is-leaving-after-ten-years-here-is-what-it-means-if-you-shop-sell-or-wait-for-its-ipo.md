*The man who ran India's biggest online store through the Walmart takeover is leaving, and the company has not said why.*

On Wednesday, November 25, Flipkart Group said its CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy will step down. He became CEO in **January 2017**, the first person outside the founding team to run the company, and stayed on as group CEO after Walmart bought about **77%** of Flipkart for roughly **$16 billion** in 2018, as [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/leadership/kalyan-krishnamurthy-steps-down-as-flipkart-ceo-after-11-years-article/3001024) and [Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/walmart-owned-flipkarts-kalyan-krishnamurthy-steps-down-ceo/) both recount. His last day is **December 4**, according to an internal memo seen by [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/kalyan-krishnamurthy-to-exit-flipkart-after-nearly-a-decade-at-the-helm/).

Chief product and technology officer **Balaji Thiagarajan** will lead the company with the board until a successor is named "in the coming weeks". Flipkart gave no reason for the exit. Three sources told [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/kalyan-krishnamurthy-exit-how-walmarts-kickback-probe-ipo-delay-shook-up-flipkart/) that Walmart asked him to step down. Neither company has confirmed that.

A CEO change at a company you do not own shares in can feel far away. It is not, because Flipkart touches a lot of people in different ways. So instead of one story, here are four short ones, depending on who you are.

## If you shop on Flipkart

Nothing changes on your phone today. Orders, returns and the Flipkart Minutes quick delivery service keep running, and nobody has announced any change to prices or policies.

What could change is direction. Walmart, sources told Inc42, was unhappy with costly bets on side businesses like food delivery and microdramas (very short, phone sized TV serials), and with how the Big Billion Days sale went. Flipkart had been testing food delivery, as we covered when it [first announced its plan](/explainers/flipkart-announces-its-plan-to-enter-food-delivery-market). A boss told to cut losses usually cuts experiments first. So if you were hoping for one more app to order dinner from, that hope is shakier than it was a week ago.

## If you sell on Flipkart

This is where the story gets uncomfortable. In late October, [Moneycontrol reported](https://upstox.com/news/business-news/latest-updates/walmart-probes-alleged-kickbacks-at-flipkart-exceeding-150-crore-report/article-202684/) that Walmart had opened an investigation after whistleblower complaints. The claim is that some Flipkart employees took more than **₹150 crore**, about $16 million, from vendors in return for favours such as better visibility on the platform. A kickback is simply a secret payment to an insider to tilt a decision your way. Think of a shopkeeper paying the mall guard to send customers to his shop and not the one next door.

The first reports named the smartphone and the luggage and travel accessories teams. Inc42's sources now say the probe has spread to Myntra and Cleartrip, two other group companies. Moneycontrol called it Walmart's **third** such investigation at Flipkart in under three years. The earlier two led to at least three dismissals and one resignation. The full amount has not been established, and no findings have been made public.

Flipkart told [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/walmart-probes-flipkart-employees-over-alleged-%E2%82%B9150-cr-kickbacks-report/) it "has robust governance processes and controls" and takes concerns seriously. For honest sellers, a crackdown could be good news: visibility you cannot buy under the table is visibility you compete for fairly.

Here is how the year built up to this week.

```timeline
March 2026 | Flipkart completes its move of the parent company from Singapore to India, a step before listing. Group CFO Sriram Venkataraman announces his exit
May 2026 | Walmart reportedly asks Flipkart to break even on EBITDA by the end of FY27, per Moneycontrol
July 2026 | Krishnamurthy says Flipkart has no IPO timeline
October 2026 | FY26 results show a ₹8,468 crore loss. More than 1,000 former employees write to Walmart's board about their stock options. The kickback probe is reported
November 25, 2026 | Flipkart says Krishnamurthy will step down
December 4, 2026 | His last day, per an internal memo
```

## If you hold Flipkart stock options

Thousands of current and former Flipkart staff were paid partly in ESOPs, or employee stock options. These are rights to buy company shares cheaply, which only turn into real money when someone buys those shares from you, usually after a listing.

That is the problem. In October, more than **1,000** former employees approached Walmart's board asking for equal access to the share buybacks offered to current staff, as [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/walmart-probes-flipkart-employees-over-alleged-%E2%82%B9150-cr-kickbacks-report/) and [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/flipkart-ceo-kalyan-krishnamurthy-is-leaving-walmarts-india-unit-after-11-years/) report. Sources say some senior people left partly because they saw no clear way to cash out. At least **14** vice presidents and senior vice presidents have left in about a year, per Moneycontrol figures cited by Reuters and Inc42.

A new CEO does not fix this by itself. Only a listing or a fresh buyback does, and both now depend on the next point.

## If you are waiting to buy Flipkart shares

Flipkart moved its parent company home to India in March, a step it needed before listing here. It was expected to list in late 2026 or early 2027. That now looks unlikely. Walmart reportedly paused a pre-IPO funding round of about **$2.5 billion** in May and wants the listing pushed to at least **2028**, according to reports cited by Inc42. Walmart has also reportedly brought in consultants McKinsey to check whether Flipkart is ready to list.

The condition Walmart has reportedly set is EBITDA break even by **March 2027**, per Moneycontrol as cited by [BW Retail World](https://bwretailworld.com/sector/e-commerce-marketplaces/flipkart-revenue-rises-18-in-fy26-net-loss-at-rs-8468-cro) and Inc42. EBITDA is profit before interest, tax and the slow write down of assets, roughly the money the business makes from running itself. Here is the gap it has to close:

```stats
₹1,45,880 cr | Flipkart Group revenue in FY26, up 18%
₹8,468 cr | Net loss in FY26, down only from ₹8,646 crore
₹1,209 cr | Finance costs in FY26, up from ₹649 crore a year earlier
```

Revenue grew by about a fifth, yet the loss barely moved. Part of the reason, per BW Retail World, is that interest and other finance costs nearly doubled. If you plan to judge the IPO when it finally comes, our guide on [how to read an IPO prospectus](/blog/how-to-read-an-ipo-prospectus) shows where these numbers hide. And if you want a sense of how a delay can reshape a startup's price, [Zepto's valuation story](/explainers/zeptos-revenue-doubled-and-its-ipo-valuation-still-got-cut-nearly-in-half) is a useful comparison.

## And if you are Walmart

On the way out, the tone stayed warm. "Both Flipkart and Walmart are better because of Kalyan," said Chris Nicholas, who runs Walmart International, praising "the governance he instilled". Krishnamurthy said "strong ethics, integrity and high standards of governance" had been central to Flipkart's growth, and Flipkart says the business grew more than **fivefold** on his watch.

Walmart now has to find someone who can cut losses, finish a messy investigation and keep a platform of more than **500 million** registered users, per Reuters, loyal against Amazon, Meesho and the quick commerce apps, all at once. As Redseer founder Anil Kumar told Reuters, "it's going to be difficult for anyone to come and step in so easily." For now, the one thing Flipkart has promised is a name in the coming weeks.
