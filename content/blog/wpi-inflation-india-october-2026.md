**The short answer:** India's wholesale inflation rose to 12.29% in October 2026, up from 11.39% in September, mainly because fuel and power prices were 32.84% higher than a year earlier. It is now about twice the retail inflation rate of 6.09%, and that gap is the part worth understanding, because some of it tends to reach your bills later.

The numbers come from provisional data released on Monday, 16 November 2026, by the Office of the Economic Adviser in the commerce ministry, as reported by [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/wholesale-inflation-rises-to-12-29-in-october-on-higher-fuel-power-prices/) and [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/business/wpi-inflation-rises-to-12-dot-29-percent-in-october-on-sharp-spike-in-fuel-crude-prices-enn26111602328). This post explains what drove the jump, why wholesale and retail inflation are so far apart, and what to watch next. It is a news summary written on 18 November 2026, not financial advice. The October figure is provisional and can be revised.

## What exactly did the October WPI data show?

The Wholesale Price Index (WPI) tracks what goods cost at the first point of bulk sale: at the factory gate, the mine or the mandi. The all-commodities index rose to 113.3 in October from 112.5 in September. Compared with October 2025, that works out to 12.29% inflation.

Here are the main groups, from the official release as reported by [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/business/wpi-inflation-rises-to-12-dot-29-percent-in-october-on-sharp-spike-in-fuel-crude-prices-enn26111602328), [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/business/economy/wpi-inflation-hits-series-high-12-29-percent-in-october-as-fuel-prices-surge-article/3000572) and [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/wholesale-inflation-rises-to-1229-in-october-2026-as-fuel-and-power-prices-accelerate):

| Group | September 2026 | October 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All commodities (headline WPI) | 11.39% | 12.29% |
| Fuel and power | 25.85% | 32.84% |
| Crude petroleum and natural gas | 57.32% | 70.80% |
| Mineral oils (petrol, diesel and similar) | 40.02% | 45.57% |
| Electricity | -0.43% | 8.06% |
| Primary articles | 8.67% | 9.24% |
| Food articles | 8.14% | 8.26% |
| Manufactured products | 9.76% | 9.54% |
| WPI Food Index | 8.66% | 8.58% |

A few other numbers stand out. Inside manufacturing, chemicals were up 17.53% and basic metals 17.21%, according to both ETV Bharat and Forbes India. For April to October, wholesale inflation averaged 10.14%, against -0.30% in the same seven months a year earlier. That means prices at the wholesale level have gone from roughly flat to rising by double digits in about a year.

One caution: the October figure was compiled with a weighted response rate of 81.1%, as [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/wholesale-inflation-rises-to-12-29-in-october-on-higher-fuel-power-prices/) notes, so it can still change. August's provisional number, 9.92%, was later confirmed unchanged.

## Why did wholesale inflation jump in October?

Almost all of it was energy. Rahul Agrawal of the ratings agency ICRA told [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/business/economy/wpi-inflation-hits-series-high-12-29-percent-in-october-as-fuel-prices-surge-article/3000572) that fuel and power accounted for 83 basis points of the 90 basis point rise in the headline number. In plain terms, energy explains more than nine-tenths of the month's increase.

Three things sit behind that:

- **Crude oil and gas.** Prices of crude petroleum and natural gas at the wholesale level were 70.80% higher than a year earlier. Global oil has been expensive and volatile this year. Our reel on [the Strait of Hormuz and your petrol price](/watch/the-strait-of-hormuz-33-km-that-decide-your-petrol-price) shows why a narrow strip of water matters so much.
- **Refined fuels.** Mineral oils, the group that covers refined products such as petrol and diesel sold in bulk, rose 45.57%.
- **Electricity.** Power prices swung from a small fall in September to an 8.06% rise in October.

Food was not the main story this time. Food articles rose only slightly, and the broader WPI Food Index, which makes up about a quarter of the basket according to [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/wholesale-inflation-rises-to-12-29-in-october-on-higher-fuel-power-prices/), actually eased a little. Manufactured goods inflation also dipped slightly, though metals and chemicals remain expensive.

## Why is WPI at 12% when retail inflation is only 6%?

This is the question most people have, and it has a fairly simple answer. Retail inflation, measured by the Consumer Price Index (CPI), was 6.09% in October, as both [Oneindia](https://www.oneindia.com/india/wpi-inflation-rises-to-12-29-in-october-highest-under-new-series-what-is-driving-the-spike-8261139.html) and the ministry of statistics reported. We explained that number in detail in [what CPI inflation is and how to read India's number](/blog/what-is-cpi-inflation-india). WPI and CPI simply measure different things.

| | WPI (wholesale) | CPI (retail) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it tracks | Goods at the first bulk sale | What households pay |
| Includes services like rent, school fees, haircuts | No | Yes |
| Heavy on | Fuel, metals, chemicals, raw materials | Food, housing, everyday spending |
| Published by | Office of the Economic Adviser, commerce ministry | Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI) |
| Used by the RBI as its target | No | Yes |

Three reasons the gap is so wide right now:

1. **Fuel has a much bigger role in WPI.** A barrel of crude feeds straight into the wholesale index. In CPI, fuel is one item among hundreds, next to rent, tuition and vegetables.
2. **Pump prices have been held steady.** Daily reports on 11 and 14 November from [HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/petrol-diesel-prices-today-november-11-2026-check-latest-rates-in-delhi-mumbai-kolkata-chennai-bengaluru-hyderabad) and [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/india/petrol-diesel-prices-today-november-14-no-change-in-fuel-rates-check-city-wise-prices-in-delhi-bengaluru-and-more-articleshow-atq5xes) showed no change in petrol and diesel prices at the pump. When crude rises and pump prices do not, state-run oil companies absorb the difference, as our story on [who pays the gap when crude rises but petrol does not](/explainers/crude-is-at-113-dollars-your-petrol-price-has-not-moved-someone-is-paying-the-difference) explains.
3. **Services slow CPI down.** Rent and services make up a large part of what households spend, and those prices do not move with oil.

So a 12% WPI does not mean your monthly spending is up 12%. It means the businesses that make and move things are paying a lot more for their inputs.

## Will this reach the prices you pay?

Partly, and usually with a delay. Madan Sabnavis, chief economist at Bank of Baroda, told [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/business/economy/wpi-inflation-hits-series-high-12-29-percent-in-october-as-fuel-prices-surge-article/3000572): "This will pressurise companies into increasing their product prices." He added that companies have so far passed on only part of their higher costs, which is why core retail inflation, the measure that strips out food and fuel, could rise in the coming months.

Where you might notice it first:

- **Packaged and manufactured goods.** Chemicals feed into soaps, detergents, paints and plastics. Metals feed into appliances, cars and construction.
- **Delivery and transport.** Bulk diesel costs flow into freight, which shows up in the price of almost everything that has to be moved.
- **Your EMI, indirectly.** If cost pressure pushes retail inflation further above the RBI's 6% upper limit, the case for higher interest rates grows. Our guide on [what the repo rate is and how it changes your EMI](/blog/what-is-repo-rate-and-your-emi) walks through that chain.

The pass-through is not automatic. If demand is weak, companies may absorb costs and accept thinner margins. If oil keeps falling, some of the pressure fades before it ever reaches shelves.

## Is this the peak?

Economists think it might be, but they are not certain. ICRA's Agrawal told [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/business/economy/wpi-inflation-hits-series-high-12-29-percent-in-october-as-fuel-prices-surge-article/3000572) that he sees October as the peak, with wholesale inflation easing to about 11.6% in November and falling to single digits in January to March 2027, depending on global oil and commodity prices. Note that October came in above ICRA's own estimate of 11.8%, so forecasts here have been running low.

There is one reason for cautious hope. Brent crude traded at around $92 to $93 a barrel on 11 November, according to [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/blog/crude-oil-price-today-11-november-2026) and [HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/petrol-diesel-prices-today-november-11-2026-check-latest-rates-in-delhi-mumbai-kolkata-chennai-bengaluru-hyderabad), well below the levels of late October. Sabnavis said he expects wholesale inflation to cool if crude stays below $100. Because WPI compares prices with a year earlier, a sustained fall in oil would show up in the index within a few months. If oil spikes again, all of these forecasts move.

## What is the "new series" everyone mentions?

You will see October described as the highest reading "in the new series". That refers to a rebuild of the index this year. On 15 June 2026, the commerce ministry launched a revised WPI with 2022-23 as the base year, replacing the old 2011-12 base, as [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/centre-to-launch-new-series-wpi-producer-price-indices-on-june-15-534546-2026-06-02) and the [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/wpi-inflation-at-968-govt-launches-new-wholesale-price-series-with-2022-23-base-year) both reported.

The main changes:

- The basket grew from 697 items to 957.
- Crude petroleum and natural gas moved out of "primary articles" and into the "fuel and power" group, which is one reason fuel and power now dominates the headlines.
- Solar, wind and nuclear electricity are included for the first time, per the Free Press Journal.
- New Producer Price Indices (output, input and services) are now released alongside the WPI. The WPI itself will continue for about five years while users move to the producer price indices.

So "series high" means the highest since June 2026, not the highest ever. Comparing it with older WPI peaks is not quite like for like, because the basket and weights have changed.

## What to watch next

- **2 to 4 December 2026:** the RBI's next Monetary Policy Committee meeting, according to the [RBI's published schedule](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=62422). The RBI targets CPI, not WPI, but a double-digit wholesale number is part of the backdrop.
- **14 December 2026:** November WPI data, due the same day as November CPI.
- **Oil prices:** the biggest single swing factor for both numbers.
- **Pump prices:** any revision to petrol and diesel would move some of the wholesale shock into retail inflation quickly.
